## Corrections applied to the final draft

### 1. NEPA / FAA Order 1050.1G claim — wrong source URL
**Draft (¶192):** "…a two-year EIS under FAA Order 1050.1G — that rests on 2025 executive action the D.C. Circuit is already probing… `[Source: https://www.faa.gov/airports/pfc]`."

The claim is accurate — it matches the regulatory-political-analyst brief ("FAA Order 1050.1G… caps an EIS at 2 years/150 pages… rests on 2025 executive action that the D.C. Circuit is already probing"). But it was cited to the FAA **PFC** page, which is unrelated. Corrected to the brief's actual source: `https://esassoc.com/news-and-ideas/2025/07/faas-nepa-update-what-you-need-to-know-about-order-1050-1g/`.

### 2. TSA CRPG (May 2025) claim — cited to a press release
**Draft (¶194):** "…the TSA Checkpoint Requirements and Planning Guide (May 2025) — the governing document, **not press releases** — `[Source: …/tsa-and-dhs-st-prototype-self-service-screening-system-harry-reid]`."

The sentence explicitly disclaims press releases while citing one (the Harry Reid self-service prototype release). The CRPG reference itself is accurate (chief-engineer and regulatory briefs both cite the CRPG, May 2025). Corrected to the CRPG document: `https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/checkpoint-requirements-and-planning-guide-read-me-first-crpg-may-2025.pdf`.

## One wording divergence (no change — draft is more accurate than the brief)

**Draft (¶25, Exec-summary pt. 3):** "$35–50 billion (Phoenix/Ironbridge, for a 50-million-passenger expansion — **roughly 70 percent above 2025 volume**)."

The infrastructure-economist brief describes the same 50M target as "roughly **double** 2025 volume." With 2025 traffic at 29.01M, 50M is ~72% above (≈1.7×), not double. **The draft's "roughly 70 percent above" is the arithmetically correct figure**; the brief's "double" is loose. No change needed — the draft corrected the brief.
