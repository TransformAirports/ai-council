# Editor notes — Stage 3

**Input:** `outputs/stage2/strategist-draft-v3.md` (~5,570 words after stripping handoff notes)
**Output:** `outputs/stage3/edited-draft.md` (~4,500 words — roughly an 18–19% cut)

## What was cut

- **The Strategist's handoff notes** (marked "strip before delivery"). Their three flagged weaknesses are carried forward as inline `[FACT-CHECK: ...]` tags instead — see below.
- **Restatement and pre-announcement.** The draft's worst habit was summarizing a point immediately after making it ("That is the frame that turns...", "honesty requires naming it," "it is more honest to say so than to manufacture confidence," "This is the objection that matters most, and..."). Kept the better version of each pair, deleted the other.
- **The rebuttal section ("Why the counter-case is insufficient")** took the deepest cut — it re-argued each counter-point at nearly full length before answering it, doubling material already present in the counter-case section. Every concession, source, and turn of argument survives; the throat-clearing does not.
- **Throat-clearing openers** ("There is a quieter body of evidence that should unsettle...", "The honest read on this category is that...") trimmed to their working clause.
- **The tally paragraph** compressed from a paragraph-per-objection recap to a single accounting paragraph. No objection dropped from the ledger.

## What was deliberately kept

- The Saarinen stopwatch opening and the closing ("Draw that boundary first. Everything else is fit-out.") — nearly untouched. That frame is the report's spine.
- Short paragraphs used for weight ("That is the whole argument, written into the airport's own concrete."; "Two payments, one processing bet, gone."; "Two buildings, two eras, one lesson.").
- All evidence, all source citations, all conclusions, the exec summary's eight points, the full failure taxonomy, both dated snapshots, and every ranked recommendation. Nothing softened.
- The honest-limits language in the commercial and sustainability sections — that candor is a feature of the report, not bloat.
